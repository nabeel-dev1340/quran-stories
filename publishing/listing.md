## Description

KDP's description box takes simple HTML (bold, italic, paragraphs, lists), so you can paste this as is. Other stores take the same text without the tags.

```html
<p><b>From the very first day of creation to our beloved Prophet Muhammad ﷺ — 42 stories from the Quran, gently retold for the youngest listeners and illustrated with love.</b></p>
<p><i>The Best of Stories</i> is a treasury for bedtime, tummy time and family time. Every story is a two-page spread: a big, bright, full-page picture on one side and a short read-aloud story on the other, full of splashes and whooshes and gentle lessons that little ones remember.</p>
<p><b>Inside you will find:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>42 read-aloud stories, including Adam, Nuh and the great ark, Ibrahim, Yusuf, baby Musa on the river, Yunus and the big fish, Sulayman and the little ant, Maryam and the date palm, the sleepers of the cave, Luqman's advice and the Year of the Elephant</li>
<li>A little lesson and the Quran references with every story, so you can read the full account as your child grows</li>
<li>16 duas from the Quran in Arabic, with easy transliteration and a simple meaning</li>
<li>The 25 prophets named in the Quran</li>
<li>A "Reading together, age by age" guide for parents</li>
<li>Eight bold black, white and red pages made for newborn eyes</li>
<li>A "This book belongs to" page, which makes it a lovely gift for a new baby, an aqiqah, Ramadan or Eid</li>
</ul>
<p><b>Made with care.</b> No prophet, angel or person is pictured anywhere in the book; each story is shown through places, light, animals and nature. The Arabic duas are taken word for word from the Uthmani text of the King Fahd Complex. Stories with difficult moments are told softly, to suit little ears.</p>
<p>Perfect for Muslim babies, toddlers and preschoolers, new parents and grandparents, Islamic weekend schools and nurseries.</p>
```

## Categories

**KDP:** choose up to 3 categories in KDP's category picker. Search it for **Islam** under *Children's Books › Religions*, then add two close matches such as a children's religious-fiction category and a bedtime-stories category. Category names change, so pick what the picker actually offers.

**Etsy:** Books, Movies & Music › Books › Ebooks (for the PDF and EPUB download).

## Prices

| Format | Suggested price | Approximate earnings per sale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paperback, KDP **premium colour** (recommended) | $19.99 | about $4.10. Printing costs about $1.00 + $0.065 per page = $7.89, and you get 60% of the price minus printing. |
| Paperback, KDP standard colour | $14.99 | about $5.29 (printing about $3.70). The paper is thinner and colours are less rich, so order a proof before choosing it. |
| Kindle ebook | $6.99 | 70% of the price minus a delivery fee of $0.15 per MB. You must choose a price between $2.99 and $12.99 and at least 20% below the paperback price to get 70%. If KDP's converted file is large, the 35% option can pay more; KDP shows both after you upload. |
| Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play | $6.99 | roughly 52–70% depending on the store |
| Your own PDF + EPUB download (Etsy, Payhip, Gumroad) | $8.99 | price minus platform and payment fees |

Order a printed proof from KDP before you publish. It usually costs about the printing cost plus shipping.

## Answers for the store forms

- **AI-generated content (KDP asks for text and images separately):** the stories and illustrations were generated with an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic), with you directing them. Answer **Yes** for text and **Yes** for images, and choose the option that matches how much you edit before publishing. KDP removes books that should have disclosed this and didn't. Amazon does not show this answer to buyers.
- **Publishing rights:** "I own the copyright and I hold the necessary publishing rights."
- **Adult content:** No.
- **Low-content book:** No.
- **ISBN:** take KDP's free ISBN for the paperback (the imprint will show as "Independently published"). Kindle ebooks don't need one. IngramSpark needs your own ISBN.
