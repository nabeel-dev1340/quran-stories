# Where to sell *The Best of Stories*

*Researched 21 October 2026 for an individual author based in Pakistan. Rules and fees change often, so re-check the linked pages before you sign up. "Unverified" means I found no official or recent confirmation. Examples use the prices in `listing.md`: $22.99 premium-colour or $16.99 standard-colour paperback, $6.99 ebook, $8.99 PDF.*

## Best plan for you

1. **Amazon KDP: paperback and Kindle ebook, not in KDP Select.** Biggest reach (US, UK, EU, Japan, Canada, Australia), and KDP pays in PKR straight into a Pakistani bank [1][11].
2. **Etsy: PDF and EPUB as instant downloads.** Buyers worldwide, and Etsy officially accepts Pakistan-based sellers through Payoneer [41].
3. **PublishDrive free plan: the fixed-layout EPUB to Apple Books, Kobo and Barnes & Noble.** One title free, no commission and Payoneer payouts. Going to these stores directly from Pakistan is closed or unverified [26][36][37][38].
4. **Pakistan direct: a local print run sold through Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, Daraz and schools.** No import costs, and most Pakistani online orders are cash on delivery [51][52].
5. **Your own checkout link for the PDF and EPUB (Lemon Squeezy).** Sell from social posts in any country and keep about 87% of the price. Its docs list Pakistan for bank payouts [45].
6. **Islamic trade channels, once the paperback has reviews:** Kube Publishing's distribution service (UK), The Islamic Book Cafe and Noorart (US) [67][68][69].
7. **Google Play Books, but only if Pakistan is an eligible seller country.** It sells in India, Indonesia, Malaysia and the Gulf. I could not confirm Pakistan [31][32][34].

Put "no prophet, angel or person is pictured" in every description. It is a strong selling point in this market.

## US, UK & Europe

- **Amazon first:** paperback and Kindle. For 70%, price the ebook at $2.99–$12.99 and at least 20% below the paperback [7][8].
- **Other ebook stores and PDFs:** PublishDrive for Apple, Kobo and B&N [37], plus Etsy [41] and Lemon Squeezy [45]. Add Google Play only if Pakistan qualifies [31].
- **Islamic trade:** Kube Publishing distributes other publishers' books on Islam to shops, wholesalers and libraries in 40+ countries. Email a proposal; a reply is not promised [67]. The Islamic Book Cafe (US) takes catalogues but no children's books that show faces [68]. Your book shows no people, but ask whether animal faces count. Noorart supplies full-time and weekend Islamic schools and runs school book fairs; I could not confirm it takes other publishers' titles [69].
- **Hardcover later:** KDP has no 8.5×8.5 hardcover [5]. IngramSpark [18] may refuse AI content [19]. Lulu's casewrap [24] works but pays only by cheque [25].
- **Skip Expanded Distribution:** 40% minus printing leaves almost nothing [3].

## Pakistan & India

**Pakistan**
- **Print locally:** offset for a few hundred copies, digital for short runs. Compare two or three printers in Lahore or Karachi. KDP copies would arrive as costly imports.
- **Sell on social media:** Instagram and Facebook pages, a WhatsApp Business catalogue and courier cash on delivery. About 94% of online orders were cash on delivery in 2022 [52]. For the PDF, take a bank transfer or Raast payment (the State Bank's free instant transfer) [53], then send the file.
- **Daraz:** individuals can open a shop with a CNIC and bank details [51].
- **Bookshops** (Liberty Books, Readings, Saeed Book Bank): I found no public scheme for independent authors. Shops have been reported to ask up to 60% of the cover price [54], so offer consignment.
- **Schools, madrassas and mosques:** bulk discounts, Ramadan and Eid gift boxes, and "sponsor a copy" bundles.
- **Your own website:** Shopify Payments doesn't work in Pakistan; local gateways such as Safepay, PayFast and XPay connect to Shopify instead (third-party report) [50].

**India**
- KDP paperbacks are not sold on Amazon.in [1]. Kindle works there, at 35% unless the book is in KDP Select [8].
- Notion Press prints in India, accepts foreign authors and pays foreign bank accounts above ₹10,000 [55]. Ask it to confirm it can pay a Pakistani account. Don't ship stock from Pakistan: India banned Pakistani imports and parcels in May 2025 [56].
- Flipkart needs an Indian PAN and bank account, and Pothi pays foreign authors by PayPal (third-party reports) [57]. Neither works from Pakistan.
- Apple Books sells only public-domain books in India and Pakistan [30].

## Gulf / Middle East

- **No KDP store:** Amazon.ae and Amazon.sa aren't KDP marketplaces [2]. UAE shoppers have been able to buy Amazon.com items through Amazon.ae's Global Store since 2017 [60], paying import shipping; check whether your book shows up there. Kindle books come from Amazon.com.
- **Ebooks and PDFs:** Google Play Books has sold books in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Jordan, Lebanon and Egypt since 2016 [34]. Add Etsy and your own link.
- **Marketplaces need a Gulf company:** noon requires a trade licence or commercial registration and a local VAT certificate [58]. Mumzworld already sells Kube's Islamic children's books; it needs a company trade licence and buys wholesale or on consignment [59]. Reach both through a Gulf distributor or reseller.
- **Bookshops** (Kinokuniya, Magrudy's, Jarir): I found no public route for independent authors, so approach the buyers with a printed copy. Printed books circulated in the UAE need Media Council approval [61], so ask any distributor to handle it.
- **Printing in the region:** Ingram prints in Sharjah [22]. Jamalon agreed in 2017 to print Lulu titles in Dubai; I could not confirm it still does [62].
- **Bulk orders:** bill shops or schools with a Payoneer payment request. The card fee is up to 3.99% + $0.49, and minimum amounts apply [49].

## Malaysia & Indonesia

- **Ebooks:** Google Play Books sells in both countries, at the older revenue split [32]. Kindle books come from Amazon.com; sell PDFs on Etsy and your own link. Apple Books doesn't sell there [30].
- **Marketplaces:** I found no Shopee or Lazada cross-border route for sellers in Pakistan (unverified) [71], and Indonesia adds rules for foreign marketplace sellers [66]. Use a local reseller who buys stock.
- **Malaysian bookshops buy through distributors.** Gerakbudaya supplies MPH, Popular and Kinokuniya and welcomes self-published authors, though its list leans to non-fiction [63]. Dakwah Corner Bookstore is a Malaysian Islamic bookshop and publisher [64].
- **Market:** English-language Islamic children's books are published and sold in Malaysia (University of Malaya study) [65]. Indonesians mostly read in Bahasa Indonesia, so license a translation to a local publisher; Gramedia takes manuscripts online [66].

## Rules to follow

- **Disclose AI where asked.** KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated text and images [10]. Apple wants an "AI Generated by" contributor and a note in the description [29]. Kobo wants AI declared as a contributor [39]. Etsy wants it in the description [43]. Google and B&N ask for nothing, according to PublishDrive [39]. IngramSpark may refuse AI content [19], and Draft2Digital refuses it without extensive human editing [27].
- **KDP Select means digital exclusivity.** For each 90-day term the book can't be sold digitally anywhere else, including your Etsy PDF. Print is not affected [9]. Stay out of Select while you sell PDFs.
- **Kindle 70% rules:** price $2.99–$12.99 (since 7 July 2026), at least 20% below print, minus a per-MB delivery fee. India pays 70% only with Select, and countries outside Amazon's 70% list pay 35% [7][8].
- **ISBNs:** KDP's free ISBN works only on KDP, each print format needs its own ISBN, and Kindle needs none [15]. IngramSpark's free ISBNs are for US publishers only [17]. The National Library of Pakistan issues 978-969 ISBNs, reportedly free [16].
- **Licences:** keep the CC BY-SA 4.0 credit for the Quran text, which allows commercial use, and the OFL font notices [70].
- **Honest listings:** say the pictures were made with AI tools, give the real page count and age range, and don't suggest scholarly review you don't have.
- **Getting paid:** the bank account name must match your KDP payee name. If KDP pays into Payoneer, Payoneer must be in KDP's payment-provider programme, a rule since 1 August 2026 [11].
- **Tax:** in KDP's tax interview (form W-8BEN), give your CNIC as your foreign tax number, because the FBR uses it as an individual's NTN [12][14]. Claim the US–Pakistan treaty: Article VIII exempts copyright royalties, and the IRS table appears to show 0% [13]. Without a tax number 30% is withheld, and the form expires after three calendar years [12]. Apple asks non-US authors for the same form [28].

## Could not verify

- Pakistan's Google Play Books seller and payment status [31]. Google's 2014 list of book-buying countries left Pakistan out [34].
- Direct Apple Books or Kobo payouts to Pakistani banks.
- Whether Pakistan, the Gulf, Malaysia or Indonesia are on KDP's 70% list. Plan on 35%.
- IngramSpark's 2026 market-access fee. Its rate card wouldn't open, so the figure comes from third parties [21].
- Whether Lemon Squeezy still pays Pakistani banks, and what happens after the Stripe move [45]. Also whether Notion Press can pay a Pakistani account.
- Etsy's one-time shop fee for Pakistan, and Daraz's commission on books.
- Routes for independent authors into Kinokuniya, Magrudy's, Jarir and Gramedia, and whether Jamalon still prints Lulu titles.
- **KDP printing cost:** KDP lists $0.065 per page for regular trims but $0.08 for large trims (more than 6.12 in wide), and 8.5×8.5 is a large trim [4]. Check KDP's calculator before setting prices.
