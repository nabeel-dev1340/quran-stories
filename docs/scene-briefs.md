# Scene briefs

One full-page illustration per story. Each brief gives the Quran references,
the moment to show, what must be in the picture and what to avoid. Read
`docs/illustration-style.md` first; its rules apply to every scene (above
all: no people, prophets or angels, ever).

---

### s01-creation — Allah Made Everything
Quran 21:30–33, 16:10–18, 2:164.
A joyful picture of the whole created world. The picture flows from **day**
on the left (sun, blue sky, clouds, green hills, trees, flowers, a river) to
**night** on the right (deep blue sky, crescent moon, stars), blending
smoothly in the middle. Include mountains, a sea or river with a fish
jumping, birds flying, butterflies and two or three cute animals (e.g. a
camel, a rabbit, a deer). Abundant and colourful.

### s02-adam — Adam and the Beautiful Garden
Quran 2:30–37, 7:19–25, 20:117–121.
The Garden (Jannah): lush and magical, with clear flowing rivers, a small
waterfall, fruit trees heavy with pomegranates, grapes, figs and dates,
flowers everywhere and gentle golden light. One **special tree** stands a
little apart in the middle ground, distinct (e.g. silvery-blue leaves with
softly glowing fruit). It is *not* an apple tree. Peaceful animals: deer,
birds, perhaps a peacock. **No serpent, no apple.**

### s03-two-sons — The Two Sons of Adam
Quran 5:27–31.
A quiet hilltop at dusk. Two offerings on two flat stones: a fine fluffy lamb
(Habil's, accepted — a soft beam of light falls on it) and a bundle of grain
stalks (Qabil's). A black **crow** perches on a nearby bare branch or rock,
looking down. Gentle, slightly thoughtful mood — lavender and peach dusk.
No violence of any kind.

### s04-nuh — Nuh and the Great Ark
Quran 11:36–44, 23:27–28, 54:11–15.
The great **wooden ark of planks and nails** (54:13) riding big rolling waves
(11:42), large, steady and safe. Animals in pairs peek out of windows and
over the deck (e.g. giraffes, elephants, lions, rabbits, birds). Rain falls
from blue-grey clouds on the left; on the right the clouds part and warm
sunlight breaks through. A mountain top can appear in the distance (al-Judi,
11:44). **No rainbow, no dove with an olive branch.**

### s05-hud — Hud and the Mighty Wind
Quran 7:65–72, 26:128–134, 46:24–25, 69:6–8, 89:6–8.
'Ad's city, "Iram of the lofty pillars": very tall carved stone pillars and
proud buildings in a red-gold desert. A huge stylised wind (spirals and
streaks of sand, a dark wind-cloud rolling in from the horizon) sweeps toward
them. On a calm hill to one side, a few small tents in peaceful light — where
Hud and the believers were kept safe. Dramatic but not frightening: ochre,
terracotta and dusty purple.

### s06-salih — Salih and the Special She-Camel
Quran 7:73–79, 11:61–68, 26:141–158, 54:27–31, 91:13.
The land of Thamud: rose-coloured sandstone cliffs with homes **carved into
the mountains** (7:74) — elegant carved facades and doorways. In front, a
beautiful, gentle **she-camel** ("the she-camel of Allah, a sign"), lovelier
than any other, with her baby camel beside her, at a well and water trough
(her drinking day, 26:155). A few palms. Warm rose and sand under a clear sky.

### s07-ibrahim-sky — Ibrahim Looks at the Sky
Quran 6:75–79.
A wide desert landscape under a sky that changes from left to right: deep
night with **one bright star** on the left, a big glowing **moon** in the
middle, and the **sun rising** in gold on the right over the dunes. (They all
set; Allah never sets.) Foreground: a small tent or a lone palm on a hill,
perhaps a few sleeping sheep. The sky gradient is the star of the picture.

### s08-cool-fire — The Fire That Became Cool
Quran 21:51–70, 37:97–98.
An enormous ring of fire made cool and safe by Allah's command ("O fire, be
coolness and safety"): the flames are drawn in **cool** colours — soft blues,
teals and mint — with a gentle glow, and inside the ring a calm garden of
grass, flowers and butterflies. Logs at the base. Night sky with stars. The
centre is empty light and flowers (no person). No idols.

### s09-four-birds — Four Birds Come Flying
Quran 2:260.
Exactly **four** different, colourful birds flying joyfully from **four
different hilltops** toward the centre, wings spread, with soft motion
trails. Bright morning sky. Shows Allah's power to give life. Nothing gory.

### s10-guests — Welcome, Guests!
Quran 11:69–73, 51:24–30.
Ibrahim's hospitality at dusk: a big welcoming tent, open, with warm light
inside; in front, a cloth spread with food — a large platter with a roasted
dish (11:69), bread, dates, grapes, a water jug — and cushions set out for
guests. Lanterns, palms, resting camels, the first stars. Warm and golden.

### s11-zamzam — Baby Ismail and the Zamzam Spring
Quran 14:37, 2:158 (details from Sahih al-Bukhari).
The barren rocky valley of Makkah. **There is no Ka'bah yet.** Two small rocky
hills — Safa on the left, Marwa on the right — with a path between them. In
the middle, fresh water **gushing up from the sand**, sparkling, forming a
small pool (Zamzam). Birds begin to circle above the water. A single tree
gives a little shade; an empty water-skin lies by the pool. Hot golden
desert, bright blue sky; the water is the focal point.

### s12-kabah — Building the Ka'bah
Quran 2:125–129, 22:26.
The Ka'bah **being built**: a cube of stacked stones about two-thirds built,
with uneven top rows, a pile of stones beside it, a stone step to stand on
(Maqam Ibrahim) and a dark stone set in one corner (the Black Stone). Rocky
hills of Makkah around, golden late-afternoon light, a few birds. **No black
kiswa** — it is under construction.

### s13-ram — The Great Ram
Quran 37:99–111.
A magnificent fluffy **ram with big curled horns** standing on a rocky
hilltop at sunrise, glowing softly — "a great sacrifice" sent by Allah. The
mood is relief and joy. Hills, desert flowers, birds, a dawn sky. **No knife,
no altar, nothing sad.**

### s14-yusuf-dream — Yusuf's Dream
Quran 12:4–6, 12:15–20.
A magical night. In the sky, **exactly eleven stars, the sun and the moon**
arranged in a graceful arc, gently bowing toward a peaceful family tent with
an olive tree (Yaqub's home). The sun is soft and dreamlike. In the
foreground, an old stone **well** with a bucket on a rope (12:10). In the
distance, a small **caravan of camels** with lanterns (12:19).

### s15-yusuf-egypt — Yusuf in Egypt
Quran 12:43–49, 12:54–56, 12:93–100.
Egypt in the years of plenty: the Nile, tall golden **wheat** fields, **seven
fat, happy cows** grazing in green pasture, big round mud-brick **granaries**
full of grain, palm trees, pyramids in the hazy distance. Sunny gold and green.

### s16-ayyub — Ayyub, the Patient One
Quran 21:83–84, 38:41–44.
A cool, sparkling **spring gushing from rocky ground** (38:42: "this is a cool
bath and a drink"), and around it dry brown land turning green — flowers,
fresh grass, a young tree in new leaf — showing health and blessings
returning. Perhaps dry, cracked earth on one side and lush growth on the
other. A few sheep or goats coming back. Soft morning light. Renewal and hope.

### s17-shuayb — Shu'ayb and the Honest Scales
Quran 7:85, 11:84–85, 26:176–183.
A market in Madyan under leafy trees (the people of the wood, *al-Aykah*): a
stall with a big **balance scale, perfectly level** — fruit on one pan,
weights on the other — baskets of dates, grain, pomegranates and figs,
measuring jars, colourful awnings, a camel or two. Warm and bright. The
balanced scale is the focus.

### s18-baby-musa — Baby Musa on the River (done — the reference scene)

### s19-musa-well — Musa Helps at the Well
Quran 28:22–28.
The well of Madyan: a stone well with a wooden frame and bucket, a stone
trough full of water, a flock of **sheep drinking happily**, and a big
**shady tree** beside it (28:24, "then he turned to the shade"). Desert
hills, warm afternoon light. Kind and calm.

### s20-musa-fire — The Light on the Mountain
Quran 20:9–24, 28:29–32, 27:7–12.
Night in the sacred valley of Tuwa beneath Mount Tur. On the slope, a single
tree glowing with a brilliant, peaceful **light** (warm gold-white, like fire
but gentle), soft rays around it. In the foreground, a pair of simple
**sandals placed neatly on a rock** (20:12, "remove your sandals") and a
wooden **staff** leaning against it (20:18). Sheep resting; a small tent far
off in the valley; a starry sky. Awe and calm. **No snake.**

### s21-sea — The Sea Opens a Path
Quran 26:61–66, 20:77–78, 2:50.
The sea split in two: two **towering walls of water** ("each part like a
great mountain") with fish, seaweed and coral visible inside them; a dry
sandy path down the middle with shells and starfish and a line of
**footprints** leading to the far shore, where the sun is rising. Spray and
sparkles on top of the walls. Awe; aqua and gold. No people, no army.

### s22-desert-gifts — Gifts in the Desert
Quran 2:57, 2:60, 7:160.
The Sinai desert: a big rock with **exactly twelve little springs** gushing
from it into twelve rivulets; soft white **clouds giving shade** (their
shadows on the sand); round, cute brown **quails** on the ground; **manna**
as sweet white-gold drops on low bushes. Twelve small tents can sit in the
distance. Mercy and refreshment.

### s23-yellow-cow — The Yellow Cow
Quran 2:67–71.
A beautiful, bright **golden-yellow cow** ("pleasing to all who see her"),
healthy and unblemished, standing proudly in a green meadow with flowers and
butterflies; a village on the hill behind. Sunny, cheerful, simple and bold.

### s24-khidr — Musa and al-Khidr
Quran 18:60–82.
"The meeting place of the two seas": two waters of different colours meeting
along a soft visible line, a big **rock** on the shore (18:63), a **fish
leaping into the sea** and leaving a magical arch-like tunnel path in the
water (18:61). A small boat in the distance; on a hill far away, a village
with a **leaning wall** about to fall (18:77). Wonder; late afternoon.

### s25-dawud — Dawud and the Singing Mountains
Quran 21:79–80, 34:10–11, 38:17–19.
Majestic mountains at sunrise with **flocks of birds** circling in harmony,
glorifying Allah together with Dawud (21:79); flowing ribbons of soft light
and sparkles weave between the mountains and the birds. Foreground: on a
rock, a softly glowing **scroll** (the Zabur) and a neatly folded coat of
**chain mail** (34:10–11, iron made soft for him). No musical instruments.

### s26-ant — Sulayman and the Little Ant
Quran 27:17–19.
A ground-level view of the valley of the ants: tall grass blades and flowers
towering overhead, an **anthill** with a round entrance, many cute **ants**
marching home, and one ant standing on a pebble with front legs raised,
calling the others in (27:18). Far away on the horizon, the **banners** of
Sulayman's great army and a little dust cloud — no soldiers drawn. Bright,
friendly morning.

### s27-hoopoe — Sulayman and the Hoopoe
Quran 27:20–44.
A **hoopoe** (pink-orange body, fan-shaped crest with black tips, black-and-
white striped wings) flying proudly across the picture with a **rolled
letter with a seal** in its beak. Far below: the kingdom of Saba' (Yemen) —
tall mud-brick tower houses, green terraced fields, a great dam, a palace —
under a big **sun** (they worshipped the sun). Adventurous and golden.

### s28-yunus — Yunus and the Big Fish
Quran 21:87–88, 37:139–146, 68:48–50.
Deep in the night sea — the "darknesses": a huge, gentle **whale** (friendly,
eyes softly closed) swimming in deep blue water; far above, the moon shining
on the surface with shafts of light filtering down; small glowing fish,
bubbles, a jellyfish, seaweed; a soft golden glow of mercy around the whale.
Optionally a tiny ship on the surface. Deep blues with soft gold.

### s29-zakariyya — Zakariyya's Prayer
Quran 3:37–41, 19:2–11.
Inside the **mihrab** at night: a beautiful arched niche with geometric tile
patterns, a hanging **oil lamp** giving warm light, a prayer rug, a **bowl of
fresh fruit** (grapes, pomegranates, figs) on a low table (Maryam's
provision, 3:37), an arched window showing stars and a crescent. An empty
wooden **cradle** with a soft blanket waits nearby (the good news of baby
Yahya). Serene: warm gold and deep blue.

### s30-maryam — Maryam and the Date Palm
Quran 19:22–26.
A quiet, remote place at dawn: a tall **date palm** heavy with ripe
golden-red dates, some dates gently falling; a little **stream** of fresh
water flowing beneath it (19:24); soft grass and flowers by the stream;
birds. A folded soft blanket may rest on the grass (no baby shown). Tender
dawn pinks and greens.

### s31-isa — The Miracles of Isa
Quran 3:49, 5:110–115.
A row of small **birds shaped from clay** on a flat stone (terracotta
colour), with some of them turning into real, colourful living birds and
flying up into the sky (by Allah's permission), sparkles along their path.
A hilly town with olive trees behind. Optionally, small and far away in the
sky, a cloth-covered **table** descending in soft light (5:114). Spring
colours, wonder. No hands.

### s32-cave — The Sleepers of the Cave
Quran 18:9–26.
Night: the mouth of a cave in a rocky mountainside, soft darkness inside (the
sleepers are not visible), and their **dog stretched out asleep at the
entrance, forelegs extended** (18:18) — sweet, peaceful, eyes closed. Moon
and stars; vines and flowers grown over the cave mouth to show long years
passing. Indigo and silver; peaceful sleep.

### s33-two-gardens — The Two Gardens
Quran 18:32–44.
**Two lush gardens** side by side, full of grapevines on trellises, bordered
by date palms, with crops between them and a sparkling **river flowing
through the middle** (18:33). Abundant greens, purple grapes, blue water.

### s34-garden-owners — The Garden Owners Who Forgot to Share
Quran 68:17–33.
An orchard of fruit trees. Show the change gently: the left part lush,
laden with fruit and glowing in dawn light; the right part the same orchard
after the night — dark, bare and withered (68:20). Empty harvest baskets wait
at the gate. Half warm, half grey-blue; calm, not scary.

### s35-dhul-qarnayn — Dhul-Qarnayn and the Great Wall
Quran 18:83–98.
Between two tall mountains, a huge wall of **iron blocks** with glowing
**molten copper** poured over the top (18:96) — orange glow and sparks. In
front, a peaceful little valley village with small houses and gardens, safe.
The sun low on the horizon. Strong and protective: steel blues and molten
orange. **Do not show Ya'juj and Ma'juj.**

### s36-luqman — Luqman's Advice to His Son
Quran 31:12–19.
A big wise old tree on a gentle hill at golden hour. On a branch, a **nest**
where a **parent bird feeds its chicks**. On the ground, a rock with a crack
showing a **tiny glowing mustard seed** inside (31:16). A path winding gently
away (walk humbly, 31:18–19) and a small cute **donkey** in the distance
(31:19). Warm, wise, gentle.

### s37-donkey — The Man, the Donkey and a Hundred Years
Quran 2:259.
On a hill above an old town in ruins (fallen walls, empty roofs): in the
foreground, under a shady tree, a cute **donkey**, alive and well, beside a
**basket of fresh figs** and a **water jug** — unspoiled after a hundred years.
New green shoots sprouting among the ruins below; sparkles around the donkey
(Allah gives life). Morning, wonder. No bones.

### s38-elephant — The Year of the Elephant
Quran 105 (Surah al-Fil).
A huge, cute **elephant** in a decorated cloth **sitting down** firmly in a
rocky valley, refusing to go on; in the distance the **Ka'bah** in the valley
of Makkah; in the sky, **flocks of small birds** (ababil) carrying tiny
pebbles in their beaks and claws, coming in waves. Dramatic orange-purple
sky. No soldiers, no violence.

### s39-iqra — Read! The First Words
Quran 96:1–5.
Night at **Jabal al-Nur**: a tall rocky mountain with the small **Cave Hira**
near the top, soft golden light pouring out of it; the valley of Makkah far
below with tiny houses and the Ka'bah; a crescent moon, stars and gentle
rays. Awe: deep blue and gold. No people, no angel.

### s40-night-journey — The Night Journey
Quran 17:1.
A majestic starry night: the **Ka'bah** at the bottom left; **al-Masjid
al-Aqsa** at the bottom right (the golden Dome of the Rock and the grey-silver
dome of al-Aqsa, cypress trees); a glowing **path of sparkles** arcing
through the sky between them and then rising from al-Aqsa up into the
heavens through layers of glowing sky. Indigo and gold. No people, no Buraq.

### s41-spider — The Cave and the Spider's Web
Quran 9:40 (spider and dove from the books of seerah).
Night at the mouth of the Cave of **Thawr**: a big, beautiful **spider web**
across the entrance, sparkling with dew in the moonlight, with a small cute
**spider** in it; a **dove** sitting on a nest with eggs on a ledge by the
entrance; a small acacia tree; moon, stars, desert mountains. Safe and calm,
silver-blue. Inside is dark; no people.

### s42-mercy — A Mercy to All the Worlds
Quran 21:107, 68:4.
Madinah on a sunny, peaceful day: the Prophet's Mosque with the **green dome**
and white minarets, palm groves, flowers; friendly animals that remind us of
his kindness — a **cat** sleeping in the shade, a **camel** resting, doves and
small birds, perhaps a little goat. Bright, gentle and warm. No people.
